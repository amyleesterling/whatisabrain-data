Data files under data/ and meshes/ are derived from open datasets and carry
those datasets' licences. Each is credited on the page.

  meshes/*.glb            Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework v3,
                          2017 structure meshes, decimated. Allen Institute
                          terms of use (https://alleninstitute.org/terms-of-use/).
                          Cite Wang Q. et al., Cell 181, 936 (2020).

  data/neurons-9.bin      Locus coeruleus noradrenaline neurons: Su Z. et al.,
  data/axons-coarse-LC-NE.bin  Nature (2026), doi 10.1038/s41586-026-11026-0,
  meshes/locus-coeruleus-*.glb  Allen Institute for Neural Dynamics, CC BY 4.0,
                          from the aind-open-data bucket (exaSPIM reconstructions
                          of 2026-05-06 and LC_percentile_meshes core_mesh.obj).
                          Built by tools/build-lc.py.

  data/neurons.*          MouseLight neurons: Janelia Research Campus,
  data/axons-coarse.bin   ml-neuronbrowser.janelia.org, CC BY-NC 4.0.
                          Cite Winnubst J. et al., Cell 179, 268 (2019).
                          SEU-ALLEN neurons: Brain Image Library,
                          doi 10.35077/g.25, CC BY-SA 4.0. Cite Peng H. et al.,
                          Nature 598, 174 (2021). The derived neuron files are
                          therefore shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 for the SEU-ALLEN
                          records and CC BY-NC 4.0 for the MouseLight records;
                          each record names its source.

  data/neurons-*.bin      ION prefrontal cortex and hippocampus projectomes: Institute
  (continued)             of Neuroscience, Chinese Academy of Sciences, via the Digital
                          Brain atlas (mouse.digital-brain.cn), dataset DOIs
                          10.12412/BSDC.1690164952.20001 and 10.12412/BSDC.1667278800.20001.
                          Cite Gao L. et al., Nat Neurosci 25, 515 (2022) and Qiu S. et
                          al., Science 383, eadj9198 (2024). Shared for research and
                          education with attribution; not for commercial use.
                          ION whole-cortex projectome (source ION-CTX): the same
                          portal, mouse.digital-brain.cn/projectome/whole-cortex.
                          Cite Gao L. et al., Neuron 114, 86 (2026),
                          doi 10.1016/j.neuron.2025.10.019. Same terms.

  data/cells.*            Allen Brain Cell Atlas, MERFISH-C57BL6J-638850, CCF
                          coordinates release 2023-12-15 and WMB taxonomy
                          CCN20230722, CC BY-NC 4.0. Cite Yao Z. et al.,
                          Nature 624, 317 (2023).

  meshes/molecules/*      Molecular structures from the RCSB Protein Data Bank
  data/molecules/*        (rcsb.org), public domain under the wwPDB policy; each
                          record names its entry, method, resolution, organisms
                          and primary citation: 5WEO (Twomey E.C. et al., Nature
                          549, 60, 2017), 4PE5 (Karakas E. and Furukawa H.,
                          Science 344, 992, 2014), 1SFC (Sutton R.B. et al.,
                          Nature 395, 347, 1998), 3BIW (Arac D. et al., Neuron
                          56, 992, 2007). Surfaces built with MolecularNodes
                          (Brady Johnston, MIT) inside ProteinBlender (Animation
                          Lab, University of Utah, GPL-3.0-or-later) in Blender
                          5.0, decimated; ligand spheres at the deposited atom
                          positions with van der Waals radii. Build:
                          tools/build-molecules.py.

No dataset here may be used commercially without the permission of its owner.
The build that produced these files is tools/build-mouse.py. The data/ folder is
hosted in the public repository github.com/amyleesterling/whatisabrain-data.
