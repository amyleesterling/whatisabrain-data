The four parts四个部分

Cell body胞体 The soma. It holds the nucleus and sums everything the dendrites bring in. About 15 micrometres across, the size of a small speck of dust. 也叫 soma。它装着细胞核，汇总树突带进来的一切。直径大约 15 微米，一粒小灰尘的大小。 Dendrites树突 The branches that take signals in, from thousands of other cells. They stay close to home, within a millimetre or so of the cell body. 接收信号的分支，来自成千上万个其他细胞。它们不会走远，都在胞体一毫米左右的范围内。 Axon轴突 The one fibre that carries the signal out. A neuron has a single axon, but it can branch and branch, and reach across the whole brain. This is what the whole-brain page draws, one line per neuron. 把信号送出去的那一根纤维。一个神经元只有一根轴突，但它可以不断分叉，横跨整个脑。整脑页面画的正是它，一个神经元一条线。 Axon terminals轴突末梢 Where the axon ends, and where it hands its signal to the next cells across synapses. Each pink point is one measured ending of this cell. 轴突的终点，也是它通过突触把信号交给下一批细胞的地方。每一个粉色的点都是这个细胞一处实测的末端。

The whole cell at true scale inside the brain. The axon runs from the cell body to the far tips; the dendrites stay close to home.

整个细胞按真实比例置于脑中。轴突从胞体一直延伸到远处的末梢；树突留在胞体附近。

Closer: the cell body with its dendrites lit and the axon stepped back. Everything that reaches this cell arrives on these branches, through thousands of synapses too small to draw at this scale. The next step shows one.

再近一点：胞体和被点亮的树突，轴突退到背景。所有到达这个细胞的信号都落在这些分支上，经由成千上万个在此比例下画不出的突触。下一步展示其中一个。

The frames on the right show a real synapse reconstructed from electron microscopy in the MICrONS volume: one cubic millimetre of primary visual cortex, imaged at a few nanometres. The orange surface is the dendrite of Aura, a layer 5 pyramidal cell, the same class as the cell drawn here and from the same cortex, though a different mouse. The blue surface is Tendril, an inhibitory axon, and the pale dot is where they touch. The cubic millimetre has the rest of that cell.

右侧的两个画面是 MICrONS 数据里由电子显微镜重建的一个真实突触：一立方毫米的初级视觉皮层，以几纳米的分辨率成像。橙色表面是 Aura 的树突，它是一个第 5 层锥体细胞，与这里画出的细胞同类、来自同一皮层，但来自另一只小鼠。蓝色表面是 Tendril，一根抑制性轴突，浅色的点是它们相触的地方。一立方毫米页面有那个细胞的其余部分。

The third frame goes a thousand times closer than the synapse: one receptor molecule, drawn from its deposited atomic coordinates. This is an AMPA-type glutamate receptor, the protein that turns the glutamate released at a synapse like the one above into an electrical signal in the dendrite. It is about 19 nanometres tall, and a synapse holds dozens of them. The structure is PDB entry 5WEO, solved by cryo-electron microscopy at 4.2 ångström resolution (Twomey et al., Nature 2017): rat GluA2 fused to mouse stargazin, its auxiliary subunit, caught with glutamate bound and the channel open. The surface is the solvent-accessible surface of the atoms, coloured by chain and built with MolecularNodes through the Animation Lab's ProteinBlender; the cream spheres are the four glutamates at their measured positions. It is not placed inside the EM synapse: where the receptors sit in that particular synapse was not measured.

第三个画面比突触又近了一千倍：一个受体分子，按它沉积的原子坐标画出。这是 AMPA 型谷氨酸受体，正是它把上面那样的突触释放的谷氨酸变成树突里的电信号。它约 19 纳米高，一个突触里有几十个。结构来自 PDB 条目 5WEO，冷冻电子显微镜解析，分辨率 4.2 埃（Twomey 等，Nature 2017）：大鼠 GluA2 与它的辅助亚基、小鼠 stargazin 的融合蛋白，在结合谷氨酸、通道打开的状态下被捕获。表面是原子的溶剂可及表面，按链着色，用 MolecularNodes 经 Animation Lab 的 ProteinBlender 生成；奶白色小球是四个谷氨酸，在它们实测的位置上。它没有被放进电镜突触里：那个突触里受体的位置并没有被测量。

What is real here这里什么是真实的

Everything. The cell is a MouseLight reconstruction from the Janelia Research Campus: one neuron in one mouse, filled with a fluorescent protein, imaged through the whole brain, and traced by hand along every branch. Its dendrites and its axon are drawn from that tracing, its cell body sits where it was found, and the pink points are the axon's measured endings. The labels are pinned to measured points on this cell, so they move with it. The only invented thing is the speed of the signal, which runs at 4 millimetres per second along the fibre, about 250 times slower than a real spike, so that you can watch it. 一切都是真实的。这个细胞是 Janelia 研究园区 MouseLight 计划的重建：一只小鼠体内的一个神经元， 被荧光蛋白填充，整脑成像，再由人沿着每一根分支手工追踪。它的树突和轴突都来自那次追踪，胞体就在被发现的位置， 粉色的点是轴突实测的末端。标签固定在这个细胞的实测点上，所以会跟着它转动。唯一虚构的是信号的速度： 它以每秒 4 毫米沿纤维前进，比真实的动作电位慢大约 250 倍，这样你才能看清。
